ABOUT MATT

Starting at the end of the bench, Matt committed to daily development — skill refinement, conditioning, film study, and competitive reps.

That commitment earned him a starting role and captainship at Vermont State University Lyndon, where he became a 500+ point scorer and consistent contributor in the North Atlantic Conference.

His journey taught him:

Now he helps serious athletes in the Mohawk Valley (NY) and Northeast Kingdom (VT) build themselves the same way.
